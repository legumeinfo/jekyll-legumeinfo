A new tool for searching gene functions curated from scientific publications is [here](/tools/search/gene_function.html). Curators from the legumeinfo team have been distilling evidence for gene functions from the literature into structured reports as represented by [this example](https://data.legumeinfo.org/Glycine/max/gene_functions/) for soybean. Ten species across eight genera currently have some representation, with the majority in *G. max* and *M. truncatula*. In addition to the full curation of gene function, gene symbols associated with gene models (currently limited to soybean) have been incorporated into the mines, allowing more convenient search. We hope to update the data available for these purposes on an ongoing basis. As always, if you have suggestions for content additions or improved functionality please [contact us](mailto:lis@ncgr.org).
